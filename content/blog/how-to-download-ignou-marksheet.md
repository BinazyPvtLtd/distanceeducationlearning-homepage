## IGNOU Marksheet Dispatch Status

The IGNOU marksheet dispatch status refers to how the original certificate progresses through the university’s preparation and delivery workflow. Generally, the process follows these steps:

Result and programme completion → Document processing → Printing and dispatch → Delivery

There is no guarantee of individual tracking in this sequence. The intervals between steps may fluctuate, especially when score or completion data need to be refreshed.

### How to Check IGNOU Marksheet Dispatch Status?

1. Confirm that the final result has been declared.
2. Verify that every program requirement has been fulfilled.
3. Check the existing information on student or academic document status.
4. Contact the relevant Regional Centre if the document remains pending.
5. When the university provides a consignment number, check the postal tracking information.

If you’re unable to find the shipment details online, contact the university through its official support channels rather than relying on unverified tracking websites.
