## GUJCET Result 2026 Grading/Scoring Details

The 2026 GUJCET Results show the performance of each candidate in every subject and the candidate's overall score. As per the information given in the results, candidates might also be able to see their percentile and/or rank.

Marks represent the score obtained in the examination. Percentile is the measure of performance of a candidate relative to other candidates. In case of Admission, merit positions or ranks are considered, wherever, applicable, as per the Rules of the concerned Competent Authority.

Candidates are requested not to fix a relationship between marks and percentile or to apply any calculation formula for percentile. The method and criteria for admission should be clear from the relevant official notification.

## GUJCET Result 2026 – Marks vs Percentile

Terms like mark, percentile, rank and scorecard depict examination performance in different ways.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Marks | Marks obtained in the examination |
| Percentile | Relative performance compared with other candidates |
| Rank | Position used for admission where applicable |
| Scorecard | Document displaying result and examination details |

Percentage and percentile are not interchangeable. Percentage refers to marks expressed in relation to the maximum possible marks. Percentile indicates relative standing of a candidate within a group. Hence a candidate should not substitute the figures shown on the official GUJCET 2026 result and must use the marks as they are.
