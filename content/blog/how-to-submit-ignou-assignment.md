# How to Submit IGNOU Assignment: Online & Offline Steps

IGNOU assignment requirements and submission arrangements can vary by programme, course and session. Before preparing your work, check the latest instructions from the university and your Regional or Study Centre. Use the submission route and deadline that apply to your course, and keep proof that your assignment was received.

## Check the current assignment instructions

Download the assignment questions for the correct programme, course code and session from IGNOU's official website. Read the submission notice for your Regional Centre or Study Centre: it explains the accepted mode, deadline, file or paper requirements and any local instructions. An older notice may no longer apply.

## Prepare your assignment

- Confirm each course code and answer the questions for the correct session.
- Follow the current instructions for handwriting or typed work, formatting and page limits.
- Add the required student, programme, course and study-centre details to each assignment.
- Review your answers, label pages clearly and keep a complete copy for yourself.
- Do not include original documents unless the official instructions explicitly require them.

## Submit online when instructed

1. Open the official submission link specified by IGNOU or your Regional Centre.
2. Sign in or enter the requested enrolment and course details.
3. Upload each assignment in the accepted format and within any file-size limits.
4. Review the selected files and details before submitting.
5. Save the acknowledgement, confirmation number or screenshot for your records.

Only use an online route published by IGNOU or your centre. A third-party upload link is not proof of an authorised submission channel.

## Submit at a centre when instructed

If your instructions require physical submission, confirm the designated Study Centre, its operating arrangements and the applicable deadline. Submit the assignment to the authorised person or office and request a dated receipt or other acknowledgement. Keep a copy of the completed work and receipt.

## Check receipt and marks

Use the official student or grade-card services to check for assignment marks after processing. Updates may take time. If a submission or mark is missing, contact the Study Centre or Regional Centre with your enrolment details and acknowledgement. Never send passwords or one-time codes in a public message.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I submit an IGNOU assignment online?

Online submission is available only where the current instructions for your programme and centre permit it. Follow the official link and process given for your session.

### Can I email my assignment to the Study Centre?

Use email only if the official instructions explicitly list it as an accepted method. Otherwise, submit through the authorised portal or centre.

### How do I know that my assignment was received?

Save the portal acknowledgement or obtain a dated receipt from the authorised centre. Keep it until the marks appear in your official record.

### Where can I check the deadline?

Check the latest assignment notice on IGNOU's official website and confirm any programme or centre-specific directions with your Regional or Study Centre.
