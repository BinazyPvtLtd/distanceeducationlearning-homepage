# **What Is ABC ID Card? Benefits, Registration & Download**

Students are now seeing the term ‘what is abc id’ while filling out various admission and academic registration forms. An ABC ID is an identity number for the Academic Bank of Credits which allows the institution to maintain and manage academic credits in a digital format. The integration of APAAR with Academic Bank of Credits, means students would have a digital academic record to carry over to other institutions if they so choose. The ABC ID or ABC number will continue to be used in various institutional forms. So having an understanding of ‘what is abc id’ will help explain how and where it’s used in higher education processes to students.

## **What Is ABC ID?**

In simple terms, what is an abc id? An ABC ID is a student's unique academic identification that is associated with their Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) account. It helps academic credits and records to be linked with the student so that they can be accumulated, managed and transferred to other participating institutions. The existing system states that APAAR is a lifelong academic identity integrated with ABC and DigiLocker. As such, students may notice references to APAAR ID and ABC ID being used interchangeably.

An ABC ID is not the same as a physical college ID. The ABC ID means something different from the college ID and has to do more with the digital management of academic identification and credit rather than access to a campus.

### **ABC ID Full Form**

The full form of ABC ID is Academic Bank of Credits, and APAAR stands for Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry. ABC is a framework for credit management, and APAAR offers a unified student identity with ABC.

## **What Is ABC ID Number?**

The ABC number or ABC ID number is the student’s academic account number in the ABC system. Students may be required to provide their ABC number or ABC ID number while processing admissions, credit transfer or other academic activities. The current official information states that APAAR is a student ID number that is unique to the student and is 12 digits in length, linked to ABC. Therefore, students are to use the number in their official academic profile, card, or academic ID rather than generate a number themselves.

## **ABC ID Meaning and Importance for Students**

To understand the meaning of abc id is to comprehend why it is important. abc id is related to digital management of academic records; credits can be built up and transferred where they are permitted, academic achievements can be integrated to student’s identity, and change of institutions can be facilitated. According to APAAR’s official platform, the system supports credit transfer and academic flexibility, and also provides access and verifies academic record.

In practical terms, **ABC is** part of a wider digital academic ecosystem rather than simply another student number. The terms **abc id means** and **abc id meaning** therefore refer to an academic identity connected with a student's credit record.

## **What Is the Identity Value in ABC Card?**

The ABC card registration form shows fields for identity type and identity value. Some students are confused about identity value. The identity value is not a unique number assigned to all students. The value is decided by the preferred identifier/option supplied by the school/college, e.g. roll number, registration number, admission number, enrollment number, etc. According to the recently updated instructions of IIT Delhi, the identity value for students of IIT Delhi is its institutional entry number.

### **Identity Value in ABC Card Meaning**

The identity value in ABC ID registration should correspond to the identity type selected in the form. Follow your institution's instructions for the roll, registration or enrollment number to enter.

The value of the identity in an ABC card means the value associated with the identity type filled in in the registration form. Students are expected to fill in the identity type specified by their school and provide the corresponding value rather than filling in their ABC ID.
