# How Is the IGNOU Percentage Calculated?

To calculate IGNOU percentage, first check the evaluation scheme for your programme and course. Convert each assessment score into a percentage, apply its prescribed weightage, and add the weighted scores. Use your complete official results when calculating an overall programme percentage.

This guide explains the calculation with examples and a free course-score calculator. It also explains why practical marks, course credits and CGPA conversions need separate attention.

## IGNOU Percentage Calculation Formula

For a course assessed through assignments and a term-end examination, the general calculation is **course score = (assignment percentage × assignment weight / 100) + (exam percentage × exam weight / 100)**. The two weights must total 100% for this two-component calculation.

If your course uses 30% assignment weightage and 70% examination weightage, the formula becomes **course score = (assignment percentage × 0.30) + (exam percentage × 0.70)**.

IGNOU's official [BANM-163 assignment booklet for January 2026](https://www.ignou.ac.in/viewFile/SOSS/assignments/BANM-163-E-Jan-26.pdf) specifies this 30:70 split for that course. Always check your own programme guide before applying it elsewhere.

### Example of IGNOU Percentage Calculation

Suppose a course uses the 30:70 scheme, your assignment score is 80/100 and your examination score is 70/100.

| Component | Score | Weightage | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assignment | 80/100 | 30% | 80 × 0.30 = 24 |
| Term-end examination | 70/100 | 70% | 70 × 0.70 = 49 |
| Final course score | — | 100% | 24 + 49 = 73/100 |

The estimated course percentage is **73%**. A simple average of 80 and 70 would give 75%, which does not apply to this weighted example.

If a component is marked out of a different maximum, normalise it first: **component percentage = marks obtained / maximum marks × 100**. For example, 40/50 becomes 80%. Do not apply the weightage twice if a statement already shows weighted contributions.

## How IGNOU Marks Are Calculated

Your course may include assignments, theory examinations, practical work, projects or other assessments. Identify the required components and their weightages from the applicable guide before combining marks.

An estimated weighted score does not establish that you have passed. Separate passing requirements can apply to individual components. For example, the official [MASS evaluation guidance](https://sites.google.com/ignou.ac.in/mass/about/evaluation) specifies separate minimum scores in assignments and theory examinations for its theory courses. Use the requirements for your own programme rather than borrowing a pass mark from another course.

2. Convert your assignment marks into a percentage out of 100.

## How to Calculate IGNOU Grade Card Percentage

Use your grade card to identify course marks and completion status. Check whether the displayed marks are raw scores, percentages or already weighted results before calculating anything.

1. Open the [official IGNOU grade card portal](https://gradecard.ignou.ac.in/).
2. Select the relevant grade card option and programme, then enter the requested enrolment details.
3. Check each required course and assessment component.
4. Match the marks to the evaluation scheme for your programme and admission cycle.
5. Calculate each completed course score using the correct weights.
6. Apply your programme's prescribed aggregation method to calculate the overall result.

If a required result is missing, treat your calculation as provisional. A blank mark is not automatically zero. Check the course status and contact the appropriate university support channel when a result appears incorrect. ## IGNOU Result Calculator: Overall Percentage

A course-score calculator and an overall result calculator answer different questions. The first combines assessments within one course. The second combines the final results of the courses that count towards your programme.

If the official scheme aggregates marks directly, the mathematical percentage is **total marks obtained / total maximum marks × 100**, using comparable final marks for all included courses. If it specifies equal weighting for course percentages, calculate their arithmetic average. If it specifies credit weighting, use **sum of (course percentage × course credits) / sum of course credits**.

These are alternative mathematical methods, not interchangeable IGNOU rules. Use only the method prescribed for your programme. Do not add raw assignment marks to a final course score that already includes them.

### Overall Percentage Example

Assume a hypothetical programme requires three courses with equal maximum marks and equal weighting. Their completed final scores are 73, 65 and 82 out of 100. The overall percentage is **(73 + 65 + 82) / 300 × 100 = 73.33%**.

If those courses instead carry 4, 4 and 8 credits, and the applicable scheme explicitly requires credit-weighted percentages, the calculation would be **(73 × 4 + 65 × 4 + 82 × 8) / 16 = 75.50%**. This illustrates why checking the aggregation rule matters.

## IGNOU BCA Percentage Calculator

BCA calculations may require attention to theory, assignments, laboratory work and projects, according to the course scheme. A two-component assignment-and-exam calculator is suitable only for courses whose evaluation actually follows that structure.

For a course with additional assessments, normalise each component's marks, multiply by its specified weight, and add all required contributions. Check practical and project completion separately where required. Confirm the final result with your official grade card.

## IGNOU CGPA Calculator: Conversion to Percentage

CGPA expresses performance on a grade-point scale. Percentage expresses marks out of 100. A letter grade, a grade point and a percentage are different representations of academic performance.

Use a CGPA-to-percentage conversion only when your programme's official guidance provides the applicable formula. Do not automatically multiply CGPA by 9.5 or 10, or substitute a formula from another institution. This page does not offer a universal CGPA conversion because the relevant programme rule must be established first.

| Factor | CGPA | Percentage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning | Cumulative grade point average | Marks expressed out of 100 |
| Input | Grade points and the prescribed weighting | Marks or an authorised conversion |
| Required check | Applicable grade-point scale | Applicable aggregation or conversion rule |

## IGNOU Grading System and Percentage Range

Refer to your programme guide for letter-grade ranges, grade points, divisions and rounding rules. A grade table for one programme should not be treated as the official scale for all IGNOU programmes.

When entering a result on an application form, use the university's result and the receiving institution's instructions. Keep extra precision during intermediate calculations and follow the applicable rounding rule for the final figure.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Applying 30:70 to a course without confirming its evaluation scheme.
- Combining marks out of different maximums without converting them first.
- Counting an assignment twice when the final course score already includes it.
- Omitting required practical, project or course results.
- Treating missing results as zero or declaring an incomplete result final.
- Assuming a good combined score satisfies separate component passing requirements.
- Using an equal average when the programme requires another aggregation method.
- Converting CGPA using a formula that has not been authorised for your programme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I calculate IGNOU percentage from a grade card?

Check the required components for each course, convert raw marks to comparable percentages where needed, and apply the prescribed weightages. Then combine completed course scores using your programme's official aggregation method.

### What is the formula to calculate IGNOU percentage?

For a two-component course, multiply each component percentage by its weightage and add the results. Under a confirmed 30:70 scheme, the course score is assignment percentage × 0.30 + exam percentage × 0.70. Other schemes require their own weights.

### Can I calculate my IGNOU percentage online for free?

Yes. The free calculator on this page estimates a course score from assignment and examination percentages. Confirm the weightage before using it. It does not determine official pass status or a complete programme result.

### How are assignment and term-end examination marks combined?

Each component contributes according to the evaluation scheme. With 80% in assignments and 70% in examinations under a 30:70 scheme, the weighted contributions are 24 and 49, giving a course score of 73%.

### How do I calculate marks for each subject?

Identify all assessments for the course. Convert each score to a percentage if required, apply the correct component weight, and add the contributions. Check any separate passing conditions as well.

### What is an IGNOU result calculator?

It is a tool for estimating academic results from entered scores. Check whether a tool calculates a single course or a whole programme, which components it supports, and how it weights the inputs before relying on the estimate.

### How can I calculate my IGNOU BA percentage?

Use the guide for your exact BA programme and course codes. Calculate the required course results first, then apply the prescribed overall aggregation method. The calculator here supports individual assignment-and-theory courses only.

### Is there an IGNOU BCA percentage calculator on this page?

The calculator can estimate a BCA course only if its entire evaluation uses the two supported components. It does not include separate laboratory or project assessments. Calculate those under the relevant course scheme.

### Can I use the calculator for all IGNOU courses?

No. It supports two-component assignment-and-examination calculations. Courses with additional assessments or different result systems need calculations based on their own evaluation rules.

### Can I calculate a final percentage when some marks are missing?

You can estimate performance in completed courses, but missing required results prevent a confirmed final programme calculation. Check the grade card status and wait for or enquire about pending results.

### How do I convert IGNOU CGPA into percentage?

Use the conversion formula authorised for your programme. Do not assume a universal multiplier. If your guide does not state a conversion method, ask the university or the institution requesting the percentage.

### Do I need an IGNOU percentage calculator app?

No app is needed to use the browser calculator on this page. You can also use the formula manually. Compare any third-party tool's calculation method with the official programme rules before using its result.
